Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FS NEXUS LTD (“FS Nexus”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you visit our website, contact us, use our services or are contacted by us in a business-to-business context.

1. Who we are

FS NEXUS LTD is the data controller responsible for the personal information described in this Privacy Policy.

  • Company: FS NEXUS LTD
  • Company number: 17309155
  • Registered office: 15 Hitchin Road, Stotfold, Hitchin, England, SG5 4HL
  • Privacy contact: info@fsnexus.com
  • Website: https://fsnexus.com

If you have questions about this policy or how we use personal information, you can contact us using the email address above.

2. Personal information we collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with us.

Website enquiries. When you submit our website enquiry form, we may collect your name, company or organisation name, email address, telephone number, the service or services you are interested in, information in your project description or message, and your marketing preference if you complete the optional marketing checkbox. You do not have to provide optional information, such as a telephone number or company name, unless it is necessary to deal with your particular request.

Customers and business contacts. If you become a customer, supplier or business contact, we may process relevant contact and business information, correspondence, quotation details, service information and records needed to manage the relationship and provide our services.

Business-to-business prospecting. Where we identify organisations that may have a genuine business reason to be interested in our services, we may collect relevant professional contact information. This may include a person's name, job title, business email address, company name and other business contact details. Sources may include the organisation's website, publicly available business directories, professional networking websites, Companies House or other public business records, referrals and direct communications.

We aim to use information relevant to a person's professional role and the organisation's potential business requirements. Public availability does not mean information can be used without complying with data protection and electronic marketing laws.

Website technical information. When you visit our website, technical information may be processed by our website infrastructure and security providers. Depending on the connection and security functions involved, this may include your IP address, browser and device information, request details, and security or diagnostic logs.

Information about other people. If you provide us with personal information about another person, you should ensure that you are entitled to share it with us and, where appropriate, make that person aware of this Privacy Policy.

3. How and why we use personal information

We may use personal information to:

  • respond to enquiries and requests for information;
  • discuss requirements, assess projects and prepare quotations;
  • provide, administer and support services;
  • communicate with customers, suppliers and business contacts;
  • manage business relationships and maintain appropriate records;
  • identify organisations that may have a relevant business need for our services and undertake business-to-business development where permitted by law;
  • send marketing communications where permitted by applicable law and, where required, where the recipient has given consent;
  • record marketing preferences, objections and unsubscribe requests;
  • operate, maintain, secure and troubleshoot our website;
  • prevent misuse, investigate security incidents and protect our business; and
  • comply with legal obligations and establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We only intend to use personal information for purposes relevant to the reason it was collected or another compatible and lawful purpose.

4. Our lawful bases for processing

UK data protection law requires us to have a lawful basis for processing personal information. The basis we use depends on the purpose and circumstances.

  • Contract and steps before entering into a contract: to respond to a request you make and take steps at your request before entering into a contract, such as discussing a project or preparing a quotation, or to perform a contract with you.
  • Legitimate interests: to manage business enquiries and relationships; identify relevant organisations and professional contacts for proportionate business-to-business development where permitted by marketing law; operate and secure our website and business systems; prevent misuse; protect our business; and maintain appropriate records. We consider the purpose, necessity and potential impact on individuals, including their reasonable expectations and rights.
  • Legal obligation: where we need to process, retain or disclose information to comply with a legal requirement.
  • Consent: where consent is the appropriate lawful basis, including email marketing to individuals where consent is required by applicable electronic marketing law. You can withdraw consent at any time.

You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests. You also have an absolute right to object to the use of your personal information for direct marketing.

5. Marketing communications and your choices

We may undertake business-to-business marketing and send information about FS Nexus services, updates and offers. We do this subject to applicable data protection and electronic marketing laws, including the UK GDPR and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

Marketing checkbox on our website. Our website enquiry form includes an optional marketing checkbox. It is intended to be unticked by default, and you can submit an enquiry without selecting it. The wording currently used is:

“I'd like to receive occasional emails about FS Nexus services, updates and offers. I can unsubscribe at any time.”

Agreeing to receive marketing is not a condition of submitting an enquiry or requesting a quotation.

When the form is submitted, the selected marketing preference is included in the enquiry notification email as either YES or NO, together with the enquiry details and the submission date and time in UTC. We use this as a record of the preference selected at the time of submission. A YES response records that the checkbox was selected. We rely on it as evidence of consent only where the checkbox was presented clearly, was optional and unticked by default, and the person actively selected it. A NO response does not provide marketing consent. Submitting an enquiry by itself does not constitute consent to receive marketing.

We retain relevant preference records so that we can demonstrate and respect the choice made. Where necessary, we may also retain a limited record of the checkbox wording or version in use at the time.

Other business-to-business marketing. The rules for marketing emails depend on the type of recipient. PECR's consent requirement for unsolicited marketing emails does not generally apply to corporate subscribers, such as limited companies and limited liability partnerships. Different rules apply to individual subscribers, which include sole traders and certain types of partnerships. Where we use personal information about an identifiable business contact, data protection law may also apply even where the recipient is a corporate subscriber.

Where we rely on legitimate interests for business-to-business prospecting, we consider whether the contact is relevant, whether the approach is proportionate and whether the individual's rights and reasonable expectations are respected. We do not treat a publicly available email address as automatic permission to send marketing.

We take reasonable steps to identify the type of business subscriber before sending unsolicited marketing emails. Where we cannot establish that a recipient is a corporate subscriber, we will treat the address cautiously and will not send unsolicited marketing unless we have the required consent or can lawfully rely on an applicable exception.

Unsubscribing and objecting. You can opt out of marketing at any time by using the unsubscribe method included in our marketing emails or by contacting info@fsnexus.com. We will act on unsubscribe requests and direct marketing objections. We will stop using your personal information for direct marketing when you object and update our records to prevent further marketing.

We may retain a limited suppression record, such as an email address and the fact that the person has opted out, for as long as reasonably necessary to ensure that we respect the request and do not contact them again in error.

6. Who we share personal information with

We do not sell personal information. We may share personal information with carefully selected service providers where necessary to operate our website, handle enquiries, manage business communications or provide our services. These providers may include:

  • Website hosting and infrastructure providers: supporting website availability, DNS, content delivery and security.
  • Email delivery providers: delivering messages submitted through our website enquiry form.
  • Business email and cloud productivity providers: receiving, storing and managing business email and enquiry correspondence.
  • Professional advisers: such as legal, accounting or other advisers where their services are needed.
  • Public authorities, regulators, law enforcement agencies or other third parties: where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect our rights, people or business.

Service providers processing personal information on our behalf are expected to do so only for the purposes and in accordance with the arrangements applicable to their services. Where required, we put appropriate contractual and data-protection terms in place.

We may also disclose information where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

7. International transfers

Some service providers may process or store personal information outside the United Kingdom. Where personal information is transferred outside the UK, we will take steps to ensure that the transfer is permitted under applicable UK data protection law. Depending on the destination and circumstances, this may include relying on UK adequacy regulations or putting an appropriate transfer safeguard in place, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, together with any required assessment.

You can contact info@fsnexus.com if you would like further information about relevant international transfers and the safeguards applicable to them.

8. How long we keep personal information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy. The period depends on the type of information, why we hold it and whether there is a continuing business, legal or operational need.

  • Business prospect records: reviewed periodically and generally intended to be deleted or anonymised after 12 months, unless there is a documented reason to retain them for longer.
  • Website enquiries: retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage follow-up and maintain an appropriate record of the enquiry.
  • Customer and service records: retained for the duration of the relevant relationship and afterwards for as long as reasonably necessary for service management, legal claims, accounting and other legal obligations.
  • Marketing preference records: retained for as long as reasonably necessary to demonstrate and respect the preference selected.
  • Suppression records: limited information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to ensure that an unsubscribe request or marketing objection is respected.
  • Website security and diagnostic logs: retained for a period appropriate to the relevant security, troubleshooting and operational purpose.

Where information is no longer needed, we will take reasonable steps to securely delete or anonymise it.

9. How we protect personal information

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, accidental loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. The measures are intended to reflect the nature of the information and the risks involved. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We take reasonable steps to protect the information under our control and to limit access to those who need it for legitimate business purposes.

10. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Rectification: ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Erasure: ask us to delete personal information in certain circumstances.
  • Restriction: ask us to restrict the way we use your information in certain circumstances.
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interests or to certain other processing.
  • Data portability: request information you provided to us in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format where the right applies.
  • Withdraw consent: withdraw consent where we rely on it. This does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.

You have an absolute right to object to the use of your personal information for direct marketing. If you object, we must stop using your information for that purpose.

To exercise a right, contact info@fsnexus.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding. We will respond within the time required by applicable data protection law. Some rights are subject to conditions and exemptions.

11. Where your information comes from

Where we collect personal information directly from you, we explain the relevant collection and use through this Privacy Policy and, where appropriate, at the point where you provide the information.

Where we obtain personal information about a business contact from another source, we may provide privacy information by linking to this policy or otherwise making it available when we first contact the individual. Where required by law, we will provide the information within the applicable period, normally no later than one month after obtaining the information, or at the time of our first communication with the individual if that happens sooner.

The source may include the organisation's website, a public business directory, a professional networking service, public business records, a referral or another legitimate business source. Where practicable, our internal records will identify the specific source and the date the information was obtained.

12. Automated decision-making

We do not currently make decisions about individuals based solely on automated processing, including profiling, where those decisions produce legal effects or similarly significant effects on them. If this changes, we will update this Privacy Policy and provide any additional information required by law.

13. Cookies and third-party website services

We do not intentionally use advertising or analytics cookies on this website. Our website infrastructure and security services may use technologies that are necessary to deliver, maintain or protect the website. Where a technology stores information on, or accesses information from, your device, we will apply the consent requirements that are relevant under PECR and applicable law. Technologies that are not strictly necessary will not be used in a way that requires consent before that consent has been obtained.

If the website's use of cookies or similar technologies changes, we will update the relevant information and provide any required controls or consent mechanism.

Third-party content and font providers: providing resources such as website fonts where these are loaded from external services.

Our website may contain links to websites operated by other organisations. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of those websites. When you follow an external link, you should read the privacy information provided by the relevant website.

If you have concerns about how we use your personal information, please contact us first at info@fsnexus.com, so that we can try to resolve the issue.
